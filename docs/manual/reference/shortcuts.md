## Shortcuts

Every combination of keys, and every hold and tap, in one place.

### With SEL

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[SEL]] + [[PLAY]] | start, pause, or resume the pitch progression immediately, without changing the global PLAY fade |
| [[SEL]] + [[HOME]] | generate a fresh randomized working sound and sequencer; no scene slots overwritten, current Tuning and room settings preserved. Undo with [[SEL]] + [[SAVE]] for the sound parameters |
| [[SEL]] + [[SAVE]] | back to the sound before your last scene tap or blend; again, forward. Keep SEL down until you've let go of SAVE |
| [[SEL]] + [[OCT−]] or [[OCT+]] | toggle drone I or drone II in/out (the previous LFO waveform remains selected) |
| [[SEL]] held, a knob | fine steps: a pitch knob a cent at a time, cutoff and resonance in tenths (whole-number readouts), Shift's move a cent |
| [[SEL]] held | modifies the relevant knobs and shortcuts; the active page remains visible, without a key-map overlay |
| [[SEL]] + KNOB 1 or 3 on Sub · Drive | each drone's sub wave |
| [[SEL]] + KNOB 2 on Details | the delay's type |
| [[SEL]] + KNOB 4 on Details | what goes into the reverb |

### Holds and taps

| Do | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[SAVE]] browser, choose A–F and a slot | store or load the sound and its authored sequence; factory Presets cannot be overwritten |
| hold a gesture key | increase its depth independently of pressure; releasing it keeps that percentage |
| tap an active gesture | begin its return to zero; tap again to pause, and tap once more to resume |
| white keys 1–8 | STRIP, DARK, STILL and TAIL, separately for drones I and II |
| white keys 9–16 | Tick, Chord, Snarl, Stutter, Weight, Shift, Clean and Wash |
| hold a page button | peek at its page: turn a knob, let go, and you're back |
| tap a page button again | its next page ("1/2") |
| hold [[ARP]] | toggle Swell on/off without changing pages |
| tap [[ARP]] | open Swell page 1 or cycle between its two pages |
| tap [[C#5]] (on any page) | pause or resume both LFO waves at exactly their current phase; Swell continues independently |
| hold [[C#5]] for 500 ms | stop the LFO by smoothly returning both LFO and Swell progressions to phase zero at the current LFO rate; remain paused until tapped |
| tap [[OCT−]] or [[OCT+]] | select the next active waveform for LFO I or LFO II |
| hold [[OCT−]] or [[OCT+]] for 400 ms | bypass or restore that LFO, retaining its waveform and level |
| tap [[REC]] three times | record your moves, loop them, stop |
| hold [[OCT−]] and [[OCT+]] for 5 seconds | the chip's own update mode, for a stuck install (see [Help](../help.md)) |
