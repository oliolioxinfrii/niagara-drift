## From Niagara Drift to Volcano

Volcano retains Niagara Drift's two-drone synthesis engine, modulation, effects and gestures, but changes parts of the FM-1 control workflow. This page is for players familiar with earlier Niagara Drift versions. For first-time users, begin with [How Volcano works](index.md#how-volcano-works) and [Controls](reference/controls.md).

| Earlier Niagara Drift workflow | Volcano v1.5 |
|---|---|
| White keys 1–8 recall scenes | **SAVE** opens an orbital scene browser; white keys 1–8 are reserved on ordinary pages |
| Scene banks selected through combinations involving SEL and gesture keys | In **SAVE**, KNOB 1 selects the bank, KNOB 2 the slot, KNOB 3 the action, and KNOB 4 the transition percentage |
| SELECT adjusts the LFO rate | **SELECT** navigates within a page family; **SEL + SELECT** adjusts the LFO rate |
| Pitch movement mainly through oscillators, LFOs and Wander | **SEQ** adds programmable notes, unequal event spacing and individually shaped transitions across five pages |
| Normal black-key switches on ordinary pages | On **SEQ → Sequence**, all 27 piano keys enter pitches instead |
| Older GLO and HOME page layouts | GLO has two Setup pages; holding HOME opens the temporary geological activity atlas |

The eight gesture keys remain **Tick, Chord, Snarl, Stutter, Weight, Shift, Clean and Wash**. The pitch sequencer is **RAM-only** and is not stored in scene slots.
